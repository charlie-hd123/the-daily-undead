Progression update

A simpler scoring system

Points earned in each puzzle now add to one permanent Score. It never decreases, resets or gets spent, so every successful puzzle moves you forward.

Failing ends your current Round, but not your career progress. Puzzle Solves and Highest Round stay permanent too.

Why might my Score look different?

Your Score is our best estimate from the history available. Old Points could be spent or lost, so the exact lifetime total cannot always be rebuilt. Even so, leaderboard positions closely match the original standings. Nobody’s Score has been reset.
